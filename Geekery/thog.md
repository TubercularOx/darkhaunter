# Thog

## Number of Attacks

In Strip 1339, Thog attacks Durkon twice and Belkar once.  In strip 1340, he attacks Belkar again.  One way to reconstruct this is as four attacks, which implies level 16+.  Another way is as three attacks and a cleave, which implies level 15-.

Strip 1340 shows that Durkon is down after Thog’s strike in Strip 1339. The case for Cleave is that Thog’s attack against Belkar is depicted as part of the same swing that downed Durkon. Otherwise, it is a fourth iterative attack.
