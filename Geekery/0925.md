# V's Strength

## Reeb's Reconstruction of 835

*This summarizes Reeb's post [here](https://forums.giantitp.com/showsinglepost.php?p=26287092&postcount=1368)*

V can carry up to twice V's max load and still move at up to 5' per round, potentially including while flying.

The lightest dwarf on the height and weight chart is 130 lbs.

Plate armor weighs 50 lbs.

A shield weighs 15 lbs.

This means Durkon is at least 195 lbs. This ignores other items and the possibility that Durkon is not the lightest dwarf possible.

Double max load for Strength 9 is only 180.  Double max load for Strength 10 is 200, which is enough.

## Reeb's Reconstruction of 925
  
Minimum weight for a human female is 85 lbs.

A bag of holding, which we know she has, is 15 lbs.

Leather armor is 15 lbs.

V is flying at speed, which implies no more than max load, instead of the double max load from earlier.

Therefore Haley is at least 115 lbs.

The max load for Strength 11 is 115 lbs, which is enough.
