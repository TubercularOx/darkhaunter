# A Brunchroom Brawl

## Tubercular Ox's Reconstruction

In Strip 730, a warlock tries to hit Roy with an Eldritch Blast and the blast hits Gannji instead. Enor moves from the far side of Gannji, past Gannji and Roy, and towards the warlock.  Before Enor can attack, Roy grabs him by the tail and flips him prone.

One interpretation is that Enor does either a move or charge action, passing through Roy's threatened area on the way.  This triggers an attack of opportunity that Roy uses to make a trip attempt.

Another suggestion is that it is a melee attack.  If it were a melee attack instead of an attack of opportunity, it would have to be explained why Enor did not get a chance to strike the Warlock.

Another suggestion that it is a grapple.  If it were a grapple then Roy would be grappled with Enor instead of Enor being knocked prone.
